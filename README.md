# Data Jobs Dashboard with Power BI

![Dashboard image 1](/images/Data%20jobs%20dashboard.png)

## Introduction
Built for **job seekers and career switchers** who want a clear picture of the data job market. Using a dataset of 2024 data job postings (titles, salaries, locations, perks), this dashboard answers: **Which roles are in demand, what do they pay, and where are the jobs?**

## Skills Showcased
- **Data Transformation (ETL) with Power Query:** cleaned and prepared the raw postings data for analysis
- **Implicit Measures:** built KPIs like job count and median yearly and hourly salary
- **Core Charts:** line, bar, scatter, donut and treemap visuals to compare roles and track trends
- **Geospatial Analysis:** map showing where jobs are located worldwide
- **KPI Indicators and Tables:** cards for key numbers, a table for role-by-role detail
- **Dashboard Design:** consistent layout, colors and card style so the story is easy to read
- **Interactive Reporting:** slicer to filter by job title, buttons for navigation, drill-through for detail

## Dashboard Overview

### Page 1: High-Level Market View
![Dashboard image 1](/images/Data%20jobs%20dashboard.png)

A quick snapshot of the market: **479K jobs**, a **$113K median yearly salary** and a **$47.62 median hourly rate**. It also shows the 2024 job trend, hourly vs yearly pay by role, and the most in-demand and highest-paying titles.

### Page 2: Job Title Drill-Through
![Dashboard image 2](/images/Job%20Title%20Drill%20Through%20.png)

Click **Drill Through to Job Title** to dive into one role (here, Data Analyst): salary gauges, remote share, degree and health insurance mentions, a global job map, top hiring platforms, and job types.

## Key Insights
- **Data Engineer** has the most openings (~129K), then **Data Analyst** (~113K) and **Data Scientist** (~98K).
- **Senior roles and ML Engineers** pay the most, around $145K to $156K a year.
- **Data Analyst** median pay is about **$90K/year**, but only ~**10%** of those jobs are remote.
- About **39%** of Data Analyst postings don't mention a degree.
- **LinkedIn** is the top platform for Data Analyst jobs, and **88%** are full-time.
